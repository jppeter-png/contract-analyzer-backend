PERSONAL LOAN AGREEMENT

Between Northgate Credit Union and Borrower. Principal: $12,000, fixed 7.2% APR, 48-month term.

Late Fees: A payment more than 15 days late incurs a late fee of $40, capped at one late fee per billing cycle.

Prepayment: Borrower may prepay all or part of the loan at any time with no penalty.

Grace Period: There is no grace period or cure period for any missed payment; a single missed payment constitutes default, and Lender may accelerate the full balance immediately without prior notice.
