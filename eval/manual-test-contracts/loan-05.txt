BUSINESS LINE OF CREDIT AGREEMENT

Between Founders Capital Partners and Borrower. Credit Limit: $75,000.

1. Definitions. "Obligations" means all amounts owed under this Agreement, including principal, interest, fees, and any other amounts Lender determines in good faith are owed by Borrower under this Agreement or any related document.

2. Collateral. Borrower grants Lender a security interest in all business assets, now owned or hereafter acquired, to secure payment of the Obligations.

3. Interest. Draws accrue interest at 11% APR, billed monthly.

4. Renewal. This line of credit renews annually unless either party provides 60 days' written notice of non-renewal.
