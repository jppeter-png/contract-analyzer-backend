PROFESSIONAL SERVICES AGREEMENT

Between Cartwright Advisory and Client.

1. Definitions. "Deliverables" means any work product, report, analysis, or recommendation provided by Advisor, whether in draft or final form, and whether or not formally accepted by Client.

2. Fees. Client will pay a flat project fee of $18,000, invoiced in three equal installments.

3. Acceptance. Client shall be deemed to have accepted all Deliverables, and payment for the corresponding installment becomes due, five business days after Advisor provides a Deliverable, whether or not Client has reviewed it.

4. Term. This Agreement continues until all Deliverables are provided and the final invoice is paid.
