EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

Between Alderwood Robotics and Employee, for the role of Firmware Engineer.

1. Definitions. "Company Business" means the design, manufacture, or sale of robotics or automation hardware, and any other line of business the Company may enter, announce, or acquire at any time during Employee's employment.

2. Compensation. $128,000 annual base salary, reviewed annually, with standard benefits per the employee handbook.

3. Non-Competition. For a period of 1 year following termination of employment for any reason, Employee shall not accept employment with, consult for, or hold any ownership interest in any entity engaged in Company Business.

4. Termination. Either party may terminate this Agreement with 2 weeks' written notice. Company will pay out any accrued, unused PTO upon termination as required by applicable law.

5. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the state in which Employee primarily works.
