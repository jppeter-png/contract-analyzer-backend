RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

Between Kestrel Property Group and Tenant, unit 7F.

1. Rent. Base rent is $1,950/month, due on the 1st.

2. Additional Rent. "Additional Rent" means any charge, fee, fine, or cost Landlord determines in its discretion is owed by Tenant under this Lease, including but not limited to amenity fees, administrative fees, and any charges Landlord deems necessary to protect its interest in the property. Additional Rent is due within 5 days of Landlord's written demand.

3. Default and Eviction. Failure to pay any Rent or Additional Rent within 5 days of the due date constitutes a material default, entitling Landlord to pursue eviction proceedings immediately.

4. Quiet Enjoyment. Tenant shall have quiet enjoyment of the premises, subject to Landlord's rights under this Lease.
